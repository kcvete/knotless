Knotless

Untie your mind.

A calm pause before the apps that pull you in. Breathe to untie the knots.

Coming soon to Google Play

How it works

  1. A pause before you open

    Pick the apps that pull you in. Opening one shows a short pause first: why now, and for how long. Then it is your call.

  2. Going in ties a knot

    "Not now" keeps your mind clear. Open the app anyway and a small knot ties in your head. You are never locked out.

  3. Breathe, or put it down

    A minute of slow breathing unties a knot. Phone-free time fades the newest one. Every morning starts clear.

No account. Nothing leaves your phone. No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Knotless has no internet permission.
